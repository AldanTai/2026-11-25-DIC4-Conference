# Abstract

\begin{abstract}
Households accounted for about 60\% of estimated food waste across households, food service, and retail worldwide in 2022. Generic language-model recipe suggestions may ignore actual quantities and contextual constraints. This paper proposes Food Guardian, a domain-specific AI agent integrating inventory retrieval, recipe matching, food-storage knowledge and usage analytics. The design separates language-based intent interpretation from structured tool execution and rule-based checks, providing explanations and explicit uncertainty. A planned household-scenario evaluation compares inventory-aware recommendations with a generic language-model baseline using recipe feasibility, inventory matching and reminder accuracy. The contribution is a traceable architecture and evaluation design; empirical results are pending.
\end{abstract}
\begin{IEEEkeywords}AI agent, food waste, inventory management, recipe recommendation, domain knowledge\end{IEEEkeywords}
