\section{Introduction}
Household food waste is a substantial component of global food waste: the 2024 Food Waste Index reports that households account for approximately 60\% of estimated food waste across households, food service, and retail in 2022 \cite{unep2024}. Meal planning is complicated by changing inventories, quantities and storage conditions. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration identifies date-label confusion as one contributor to consumer food waste \cite{fdafoodwaste}.

Food Guardian is proposed as a household decision-support agent rather than an unrestricted recipe chatbot. Its four design contributions are natural language interaction, agentic decision-making, food safety and context awareness, and data-driven food management \cite{dic3plan}. The objective is to connect meal suggestions to actual household records while communicating constraints and uncertainty. This draft describes the proposed architecture and evaluation protocol; it does not claim experimentally demonstrated waste reduction.
