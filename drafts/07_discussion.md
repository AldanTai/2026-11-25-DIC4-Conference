# Discussion

\section{Discussion}
The proposed architecture may improve traceability by grounding recommendations in structured records and separating factual checks from generated explanations. Its effectiveness depends on accurate inventory entry, unit normalization, recipe coverage and maintained domain knowledge. Incorrect storage information can invalidate a reminder; the system should request clarification rather than infer safety from a date alone.

A small simulated benchmark can evaluate functional behavior but cannot establish real household waste reduction. A longitudinal study with measured consumption and disposal would be needed for that claim. Other limitations include model variability, privacy of household records, and the need for local food-label guidance. These are anticipated limitations, not observations from completed experiments.
